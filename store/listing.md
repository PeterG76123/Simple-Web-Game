plus the answers for the forms under Policy > App content.

## App name (30 characters max)

Eel Escape: Puzzle & Aquarium

## Short description (80 characters max)

Slide the glowing eels free, one by one, then fill your own aquarium with fish.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Untangle the deep. Every eel faces a way out, but they're all in each other's way. Work out the right order, slide them free one by one, and clear the board.

Eel Escape is a calm, clever puzzle game with 450 levels and an aquarium of your own to build as you play.

🐍 SIMPLE TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
• Tap an eel to send it swimming out, or slide it part of the way to make room for another.
• Each eel can only leave the way its head is pointing.
• Every level can be solved, and the game tells you the fewest moves it takes.

🌊 SIX REGIONS OF THE DEEP
• Kelp Shallows: learn the ropes.
• Jellyfish Bay: see-through eels, and sliding eels part of the way so others can pass.
• Crystal Reef: eels bounce off reflectors and round corners.
• Whirlpool: turn the reflectors yourself to aim eels at a way out.
• Sunken Wreck: rest an eel on a plate to open gates of the same colour.
• Mirror Lagoon: twin eels that always move together.
Three difficulties, Easy, Medium and Hard, with 25 levels in every region.

🐠 BUILD YOUR AQUARIUM
• Earn pearls for every level you finish, and more for solving it in the fewest moves.
• Solve 3 levels a day for a daily pearl bonus that grows with your streak, with a big bonus every 7th day.
• Spend them on a goldfish tank, a tropical tank and a marine tank.
• Fill each one with fish, swaying plants and decorations, from snails and guppies to a reef shark.
• Fish school together, chase their food, and dart away if you tap them. Crabs scuttle into their cave to hide.
• Arrange everything just how you like it, then drag your finger through the water and watch the fish follow.

💡 STUCK? NO PROBLEM
• Free hints every day, and more by watching a short ad.
• Undo as many moves as you like, and your progress in every level is saved.

🎧 RELAXING BY DESIGN
• Calm underwater music and gentle sound effects.
• Glowing sea creatures drift by in the background (they can be turned off).
• No timers, no lives, no pressure.

Eel Escape is free to play, with optional ads and in-app purchases (remove ads, hint packs and pearl packs).

# Answers for Play Console's "App content" forms

## Ads

Yes, the app contains ads (Google AdMob).

## Target audience and content

- Target age groups: 13–15, 16–17, 18 and over. (Not under 13: choosing younger ages puts the app under the Families policy, which limits which ads can be shown.)
- Appeals to children? No.

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

- Category: Game (puzzle).
- Violence, fear, sexuality, language, drugs, gambling: none.
- Users can interact or share content: No.
- Shares the user's location: No.
- Digital purchases: Yes (in-app purchases).
- Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3 / Australian G.

## Data safety

Data collected (by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and RevenueCat, not by us):

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device or other IDs (advertising ID) | Yes | Yes | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| App interactions | Yes | Yes | Advertising, analytics |
| Approximate location (from IP address) | Yes | Yes | Advertising, fraud prevention |
| Purchase history | Yes | No | App functionality (unlocking and restoring purchases) |
| Crash logs / diagnostics | Yes | No | Analytics |

- Is all collected data encrypted in transit? Yes.
- Can users ask for their data to be deleted? Game progress is only on the device (deleting the app removes it). Write that, and point to the privacy policy.
- No account is needed; no personal information (name, email, contacts, photos) is collected.

- Advertising ID: Yes, used for advertising and analytics (AdMob). The app declares the AD_ID permission through the AdMob plugin.
