How to play
  • Drag a line the way its arrow points. The rest of the line follows the head.
  • Double-tap a line to send it straight out. If something is in the way, it bumps it and springs back.
  • You can stop partway, and drag a line back again, but never past where it started.
  • Solid parts block each other. Dashed parts are see-through, so other lines can pass through them.
  • Triangles are reflectors. A line that hits the slanted side turns 90° and keeps going. The flat sides block.
  • Slide every line off the board to finish the level. Every level can be solved.
  • Stuck? Press Hint and the next best move flashes.
  • Finish 10 levels in a group to unlock the next group.
