Arrow Escape

Untangle the lines, one arrow at a time.

Levels

How to play
  • Tap a line to send it sliding off the board in the direction its arrow points.
  • A line can only escape if nothing is in its way, all the way to the edge.
  • If another line is in the way, it bumps into it and you lose a life. You get 3 lives per level.
  • Clear every line to finish the level. Every level can be solved.
