What makes a good picture
  • Simple, bold shapes in flat colours, like an icon, emoji, sticker or clip art. No photos, gradients or fine detail.
  • One subject, roughly centred, on a plain white or transparent background.
  • Up to 7 colours. The Insane puzzle is a 40×40 grid, so small details disappear.
  • PNG or JPG, up to 20 MB. Any size; square is best.
  • No text, apart from big simple numbers like "2027". No famous characters or brands.
