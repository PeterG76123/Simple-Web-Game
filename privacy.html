Eel Escape: Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of release]

Eel Escape is a puzzle game. This page explains what information the game uses and why. In short: your progress stays on your device, we don't ask you to create an account, and the free version shows ads from Google, which uses some information to show and measure them.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress, settings (sound, music, sea creatures), the hints you have, and your purchases on your own device. This isn't sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) removes it.

Ads

The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, between levels and when you choose to watch one for a reward. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. How Google uses this is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice from the app's settings at any time.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the app asks permission before your device's advertising identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised that way.
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for rewards stay available.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We don't receive your payment details; we only learn which item was bought, so the game can give it to you.

Game settings fetched from our website

When it starts, the game may fetch a small settings file from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages) to adjust things like how many free hints you get. Like any website, the host may log your IP address when this file is downloaded. No information about you is sent with the request.

Children

Eel Escape is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
